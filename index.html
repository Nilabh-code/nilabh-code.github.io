Robot Atlas

Turning a personal reference into something other people can start from — an eight-step build roadmap, 57 catalogued parts, 18 platforms pulled apart. The hard part is not the specs, it is saying honestly where each one fails.

Sim-to-real on a budget

Training locomotion policies in MuJoCo and trying to transfer them to hardware built from cheap serial servos. The gap between a policy that walks in simulation and one that survives a real floor is turning out to be entirely mechanical.
